01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Learn to act when every second matters.

The Lifesaving Link is a classroom demonstration for Ontario warehouse employees. Its main lesson teaches CPR and AED awareness, with brief modules on emergency reporting and bleeding control.

◉

Recognize

Spot an unresponsive person and check for normal breathing without exposing yourself to danger.

✚

Respond

Know how to call 911, request an AED and begin appropriate CPR following current guidance.

✓

Practise

Work through a warehouse scenario, complete a quiz and identify skills to practise with an instructor.

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

By the end of the awareness training, learners should be able to…

  1. Recognize possible cardiac arrest and describe when to call 911.
  2. Describe adult hands-only CPR and the role of an AED.
  3. Explain the key AED safety prompts and actions.
  4. Identify first steps for a workplace emergency and severe bleeding.
02 / EMERGENCY ACTION PLAN

A coworker collapses. What next?

Example: an employee collapses near a warehouse loading bay. Follow the workplace plan and emergency dispatcher’s instructions.

01

Check safety

Look for forklifts, traffic, electrical hazards and falling stock before approaching.

02

Check & call 911

Check responsiveness and normal breathing. Call 911 or direct someone to call; ask another coworker to bring the AED.

03

Begin CPR

If the adult is unresponsive and not breathing normally, begin chest compressions. Follow the dispatcher’s instructions.

04

Use the AED

Turn the AED on and follow its voice prompts. Keep everyone clear during analysis or shock, and resume CPR as instructed.

CLASSROOM SCENARIO

A warehouse employee suddenly collapses.

Who will call 911? Who will retrieve the AED? How will you keep the forklift aisle safe and help responders find the entrance?
