LEARNING OBJECTIVES

After completing this program, learners should be able to…

  1. Identify at least three warehouse emergencies and immediate scene hazards.
  2. Outline the correct response sequence: protect, call 911, locate the first aider/AED, provide appropriate assistance.
  3. Recognize when CPR/AED and bleeding-control actions may be needed.
  4. Apply evacuation and reporting steps to a short workplace scenario.
02 / EMERGENCY ACTION PLAN

What to do in the first moments.

A simple action sequence to help employees avoid panic and activate the workplace response plan.

01

Check for danger

Look for moving forklifts, falling stock, electricity, chemicals, smoke and other hazards. Do not become another casualty.

02

Call for help

Call 911 for serious or life-threatening emergencies. Tell a supervisor and summon the designated workplace first aider.

03

Find equipment

Direct someone to bring the first aid kit or AED, if available. Follow dispatcher instructions and act within your training.

04

Protect & report

Keep the route clear for responders, follow evacuation instructions when required, and report the incident using workplace procedures.

WAREHOUSE SCENARIO

A coworker collapses beside a forklift aisle.

Stop traffic if safe, check the area for hazards, call 911, get an AED and follow dispatcher guidance. Have someone meet emergency responders at the entrance.
